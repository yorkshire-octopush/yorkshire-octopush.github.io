<details markdown="1">
<summary>Who can join?</summary>

Children and young people aged 8+, whether or not they have played before.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Does my child need to be a strong swimmer?</summary>

Players need to be able to swim 25 metres unaided. Everything else, including diving down and holding their breath, we teach as part of the sessions.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Is it safe?</summary>

Octopush is a limited-contact sport. The rules forbid holding, pushing or blocking other players, and players wear a padded glove and a hat with ear guards.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Do we need to buy any equipment?</summary>

No. We provide all the kit (masks, snorkels, fins, sticks, gloves and hats), so your child only needs a swimming costume and a towel.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>How much does it cost?</summary>

Your child can come and try it for free. After that, it costs £20 a month, paid by direct debit.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Can parents watch?</summary>

Yes. Parents are welcome to watch from the reception area at The Edge.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Can my child play in matches?</summary>

Yes, if they would like to. We are a relaxed club and play mainly for fun, but there are junior competitions too, and most of our players enjoy taking part. At the 2026 British Octopush Junior Regional Championship in Winchester on 14 November our teams won gold in the under-14s and silver in the under-11s and under-16s.
</details>
