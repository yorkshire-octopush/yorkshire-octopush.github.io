Yorkshire Octopush Juniors is the junior section of [Team Yorkshire Underwater Hockey Club](https://www.gbuwh.co.uk/club.php?club_id=3), for young people aged 8 to 17 from Leeds and the surrounding area. We train every Saturday afternoon at The Edge, the University of Leeds sports centre, and welcome complete beginners as well as players who already know the game.

We are a relaxed club and most of all we play for fun. There are also junior competitions, and most of our players like to take part. At the November 2026 championships our teams won **gold in the under-14s** and **silver in both the under-11s and under-16s**.
